How to refuel a bike in Vietnam: petrol and phrases at the station

At most petrol stations an attendant does everything. Your job is to switch off the engine, open the tank, say the amount or ask for a full tank and check the pump before paying.

Refuelling step by step

  1. Pull up to a free pump and switch off the engine.
  2. Open the seat or the fuel cap.
  3. Say the amount — for example 50,000 dong — or ask for a full tank.
  4. Check that the pump counter has been reset.
  5. Pay after filling, in cash or by QR if they accept it.

Useful phrases

  • Năm mươi nghìn — for 50,000 dong;
  • Đầy bình — full tank;
  • Xăng 95 — RON 95 petrol.

You can also just show the amount on your phone screen. Prices change, so look at the display at the actual station.

Which petrol to use

Follow the manual or the sticker for your model. If in doubt, ask the seller or a mechanic about your specific bike. Don't use fuel from unlabelled bottles when there's a proper petrol station nearby.

Where the tank is

On a scooter the filler is often under the seat; on a Cub/Wave it's under the seat or in the middle. Ask to be shown when you pick up the bike. Don't overfill and don't smoke near the pump.

When to refuel

Don't wait for a completely empty tank: the gauge on older bikes can be inaccurate. Before a trip out of town, fill up in Nha Trang and mark the big stations on your map. Other costs are covered in the article on bike running costs.

If the bike stalls

Move smoothly to the edge of the road and check the fuel level. If there's petrol, the cause may be technical — better to contact a mechanic than keep trying to ride.

The main thing

Choose visible stations, switch off the engine, check the pump shows zero and don't put off refuelling until the last bar.
