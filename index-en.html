Rework

What we do to every bike

We don’t touch the engine: it stays a stock 50cc with the factory design. Everything on the papers matches the bike.

Sprocket and chain

We fit a smaller rear sprocket and a new chain, tensioned properly. No slack chain — the engine pulls and the bike picks up speed.

Brakes

We fully adjust the brakes for the higher speed so the bike stops with confidence.

Oil and test ride

We change the oil, check the whole bike and test-ride it ourselves in Nha Trang before sale.

For you

Don’t crawl along the kerb — ride with traffic

A regular 50cc — an automatic or a stock semi-automatic — does about 50 km/h. It won’t climb a hill with two people: you have to get off, and sometimes it struggles even with one. Everyone overtakes you while you crawl on the right. Our bike cruises the city at 60–70 km/h and climbs hills two-up — you ride as a normal road user and actually enjoy it.

Why 50cc

No licence on a bigger bike: a fine and the impound lot

Anything over 50cc needs a motorcycle licence (class A). Without one, Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP means a fine, and the bike can be taken to the impound lot. Our bikes have 50cc written on the Blue Card — no licence needed.

50ccno licence needed
Motorcycle up to 125 cc without a licence: fine up to 4,000,000 ₫ (Art. 18, cl. 5b).
Motorcycle over 125 cc without a licence: fine up to 8,000,000 ₫ (Art. 18, cl. 7b).
Impound lot: the bike can be held for up to 7 days. You get it back only after paying the fine and the storage. If it’s a rental, you still pay — and you’re left without transport meanwhile.
