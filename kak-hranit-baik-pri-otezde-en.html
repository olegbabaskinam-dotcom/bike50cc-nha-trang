Leaving your bike for a month: storage in Vietnam

Leave a bike in the rain and sun for a few weeks, and your return may start with a flat battery, soft tyres and corroded contacts. Proper storage doesn't start with a cover, but with a dry, safe place.

Where to leave the bike

The best option is a covered, guarded car park without direct sun or flooding. Check the access hours, the price, the car park's liability and the rules for keys. Photograph the bike, the plate, the spot and the parking ticket.

Preparing before you leave

  • wash the bike and let it dry completely;
  • check the tyre pressure against the model's specs;
  • remove food, documents and valuables from the storage box;
  • fill up and follow the manufacturer's advice for the storage period;
  • lubricate locks and moving points if the service schedule calls for it;
  • lock the steering and use an extra lock.

The battery

A weak battery can go flat in a few weeks. For long storage, discuss with a mechanic disconnecting a terminal or using a suitable maintenance charger. Don't ask a friend to just start the bike for two minutes: a short idle run doesn't always restore the charge and adds condensation.

Cover and humidity

A cover should be clean, the right size and not trap water inside. Outdoors it doesn't replace a roof and can rub the plastic in the wind. In the rainy season, ventilation and no water under the wheels are especially important.

Should you ask someone to ride it

If the person rides confidently and is allowed to, a proper ride now and then can be better than a short start-up. But give the keys only to someone you trust; agree in advance on parking, fuel and what to do if something breaks.

After you return

  1. Check the floor under the bike for leaks.
  2. Check the tyres, brakes, lights and fuel level.
  3. Start the engine and let it settle without sharp throttle.
  4. Take a short calm ride near the car park.
  5. If it's hard to start or there are strange noises, go to a workshop.

A general list of regular jobs is in the article on bike care in a hot climate, and how to choose parking — in parking in Nha Trang.

In short

A covered guarded car park, a clean dry bike, checked tyres and a plan for the battery are the basis for a calm return after a month away.
