Who a 50cc bike in Nha Trang suits — and who it doesn't

We sell bikes, but there's no point selling them to everyone: someone a 50cc doesn't suit will come back disappointed, not grateful. So here is an honest filter. First who it suits, then who it doesn't.

Who a 50cc bike suits

People wintering or living in Nha Trang for months. The main scenario. Your own transport for the season is cheaper than renting and you don't have to decide every month whether to extend. The maths is in a bike for the winter season.

People without a motorcycle category on their licence. That's the whole point of 50cc: in Vietnam a motorcycle category is not required for vehicles up to 50 cm³. The caveat we always mention: ≤50 cm³ in the registration is a necessary condition, but traffic rules, helmets, age and terms of stay still apply, and the requirements should be checked at the time of purchase. More — licence rules for 50cc.

People riding around the city and nearby. Nha Trang, the seafront, markets, beaches, trips of 20–40 km — exactly the distance where a 50cc is comfortable.

Beginners on two wheels. Low power is a plus here: the bike forgives mistakes and doesn't jump out from under you. Especially an automatic.

People who need an inexpensive bike that runs well. Our 50cc bikes come with papers; before selling we change the oil and check the tyres and brakes.

Who a 50cc bike won't suit

People here for a week or two. The purchase won't pay off, and selling takes time you don't have. Rent instead.

People planning to ride between cities. Nha Trang — Da Lat, Nha Trang — Hoi An and similar routes aren't the best choice for a 50cc: the speed and power reserve aren't there, especially in the mountains and on long climbs. There's a separate guide — riding uphill on a 50cc.

People carrying two adults plus luggage every day. Two people do ride a 50cc, but on climbs and when accelerating you feel it right away. Details — in can two people ride a 50cc.

People who want speed. A 50cc is about getting around the city, not about performance. If you want to overtake traffic on the highway, you've picked the wrong class.

People not ready to do any maintenance. Oil, tyres, brakes, bulbs — minimal attention is still needed. We offer no after-sales support: once sold, the bike is yours.

People who don't feel confident in Vietnamese traffic. That's an honest stop factor, and it's better to admit it right away. Start with the basics of riding in Vietnam and decide after that.

Three questions to check yourself

  1. How many months am I here? Less than two — rent. Two or more — buying becomes better value.
  2. Where will I ride? City and surroundings — a 50cc works. Intercity highways and mountains — no.
  3. Am I ready to sell the bike myself before leaving? If yes — buying works. If not — count the cost of ownership without getting part of the money back.

If all three answers point to buying, the choice is simple: automatic or semi-automatic and which bike in stock is more comfortable for you.

Not sure? Come and have a look

Viewing by appointment with our operator, south of Nha Trang, daily 9:00–21:00. You can come, sit on the bike, ride for a couple of minutes and decide on the spot. If it doesn't suit you — no hard feelings, we don't push anyone: there are no discounts for a quick decision and nothing to hurry you with.

In short

A 50cc bike is city transport for people who live in Nha Trang for months and want to ride legally without a motorcycle category. For short stays, long highways and constant riding two-up with luggage it's not the best choice, and we prefer to say so up front.
